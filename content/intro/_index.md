So what’s the big deal about it? What makes Touhou Project so special is the unending fan love and passion for the characters, and an absurdly talented fandom that is unique to the community for over 20+ years.  The inspiration this seemingly simple game series have given its fans sends ripples throughout the gaming and anime world. Much of this is thanks to the Fan Circles (Doujin Circles) and lax copyright policies.

Touhou started in 1997, is a series of Bullet Hell (danmaku) games, a majority which is solely created by ONE man, ZUN.  

## ZUN
ZUN-san, a musician, programmer, character designer, and a developer of “Team Shanghai Alice” who created a series of Bullet hell Shoot ‘em up games known as **Touhou Project.**
He first published his Touhou series from 1997 Touhou, starting from PC-98 東方靈異伝 ~Highly Responsive to Prayers and is still publishing games to the current day.
