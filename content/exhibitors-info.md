## Exhibitors Info

Applications will remain open from now until **March 26th, 2027**. Once the application period has
been submitted, you will be contacted soon after to notify you of your application status.
Exhibitors are chosen through a juried process. Please note that reviewing our applications takes
time, so we ask for your patience during this time period.

Please keep in mind that we are a Touhou Project themed fan event, and we will be prioritizing
sellers with Touhou Project fan merchandise within their product offerings. If you currently do not
offer Touhou Project products in your portfolio, but plan on producing some in time for the event,
feel free to let us know in the form.

_**We do not permit sale of the following items at our events:**_

- Bootleg merchandise / anything that violates copyright infringement
- AI generated material or AI assisted material
- Physical weapons of any kind (blades, firearms, etc.)
- Food or beverages of any kind (outside food and beverages are permitted, but for your own personal
  consumption)

This list is not exhaustive and is subject to change at any time. If you are found selling these
items you will get one warning to remove them failure to do so will result in removal from the
convention without refund.

### Proxy Selling

We allow proxy selling on behalf of an artist, so long as permission from the artist been granted
and contracts have been written out. Please be sure to clarify in the form that you are a proxy
seller and state the name(s) of the artist in which you are selling on behalf of.

### Selling R-18/NSFW Material

R-18 (material depicting content that is sexual in nature) is allowed, but _must_ be covered,
labeled and/or sealed. Please note that if a person wishes to purchase R-18 material, the seller
MUST take the responsibility of checking for a valid government-issued ID before a purchase is
placed. Sensitive materials that may not classify as R-18 (blood, profanity, artistic nudity, etc.)
are also allowed, however we leave it up to the seller to make their best judgement for choosing the
way in which the material is displayed and labeled.

If you have any additional questions or concerns about our Vendor booths, please contact us through
our email [exhibitors@touhoufest.org](mailto:exhibitors@touhoufest.org) or leave your question in
the QnA channel on our official [Discord](https://discord.gg/touhoufest).

For all exhibitor types, extra exhibitor badges are $50 each.
