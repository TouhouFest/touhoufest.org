## Meetups
Cosplay Gatherings will take place in our beautiful Pine Wind Japanese Garden.

Our full meetup and gathering schedule will be released when we get closer to the event!

## Get the perfect photos
Our venue's Pine Wind Japanese Garden is the perfect place to get photos of your cosplays. At the end of each of the TouhouFest cosplay meetups, our photographers will offer free photos in the garden for anyone who wants them.

This year, Medukitty will be the Master of Ceremonies!

## Touhou Cosplay Guild
The Touhou Cosplay Guild Discord Server brings over 350 beginner and seasoned cosplayers together and provides learning resources for cosplaying.
It was founded by Violet Reverie, who will be joining us as one of our Cosplay Ambassadors.
