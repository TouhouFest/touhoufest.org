## 1. Introduction

1.1 This privacy policy sets out how TouhouFest ("[we/us/our]") collects, uses, stores, shares and
protects information about individuals ("personal data") in accordance with the California Consumer
Privacy Act 2018, and other applicable data protection legislation.

1.2 This policy applies where we are acting as a data controller with respect to the personal data
of our website visitors and service users; in other words, where we determine the purposes and means
of the processing of that personal data.

1.3 We use cookies on our website. Insofar as those cookies are not strictly necessary for the
provision of our website and services, we will ask you to consent to our use of cookies when you
first visit our website.

1.4 In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" refer to TouhouFest and its parent organization, The AMP
Organization. For more information about us, see Section 14.

## 2. How we use your personal data

2.1 In this Section 2 we have set out:

- the general categories of personal data that we may process;
- in the case of personal data that we did not obtain directly from you, the source and specific
  categories of that data;
- the purposes for which we may process personal data; and
- the legal bases of the processing.

2.2 We may process your account data ("account data"). The account data may include your full legal
name, email address, date of birth, phone number, ZIP code, and emergency contact info. The source
of the account data is from you when a new account is opened on our website. The account data may be
processed for the purposes of running our events, providing our services, ensuring the security of
our website and events, maintaining back-ups of our databases and communicating with you. The legal
basis for this processing is consent.

2.3 Date of birth is required during registration to ensure age-gated material at events hosted by
the organization are accessed in compliance with local laws, and to ensure that minors are
accompanied by a legal guardian. The legal basis for this processing is consent.

2.4 We may process your information included in your personal profile on our website ("profile
data"). The profile data may include full legal name, ZIP code, birth date, email address. The
profile data may be processed for the purposes of registering for . The legal basis for this
processing is consent.

2.5 We may process your personal data that are provided in the course of the use of our services
("service data"). The service data may include IP address, history of connections to our website,
and frequency and duration of connections. The service data may be processed for the purposes of
operating our website, providing our services, ensuring the security of our website and services,
maintaining back-ups of our databases and communicating with you. The legal basis for this
processing is our legitimate interests, namely the proper administration of our website and service
as well as for historical reference and statistics.

2.6 We may process information that you post for publication on our website or through our services
("publication data"). The publication data may be processed for the purposes of enabling such
publication and administering our website and services. The legal basis for this processing is
consent.

2.7 We may process information contained in any inquiry you submit to us regarding goods and/or
services ("inquiry data"). The inquiry data may be processed for the purposes of offering, marketing
and selling relevant goods and/or services to you. The legal basis for this processing is consent.

2.8 We may process information that you provide to us for the purpose of subscribing to our email
notifications and/or newsletters ("notification data"). The notification data may be processed for
the purposes of sending you the relevant notifications and/or newsletters. The legal basis for this
processing is the performance of a contract between you and us and/or taking steps, at your request,
to enter into such a contract.

2.9 We may process any of your personal data identified in this policy where necessary for the
establishment, exercise or defense of legal claims, whether in court proceedings or in an
administrative or out-of-court procedure. The legal basis for this processing is our legitimate
interests, namely the protection and assertion of our legal rights, your legal rights and the legal
rights of others.

2.10 In addition to the specific purposes for which we may process your personal data set out in
this Section 3, we may also process any of your personal data where such processing is necessary for
compliance with a legal obligation to which we are subject, or in order to protect your vital
interests or the vital interests of another natural person.

2.11 Please do not supply any other person's personal data to us, unless we prompt you to do so.

## 3. Providing your personal data to others

3.1 We may disclose your personal data to any member of our organization (this means our
subsidiaries, our ultimate holding organization and all its subsidiaries) insofar as reasonably
necessary for the purposes, and on the legal bases, set out in this policy.

3.2 Financial transactions relating to our website and services are handled by our payment services
provider, Stripe. We do not process transaction data within our systems. We will operate through our
payment service providers to the extent necessary for the purposes of processing your payments,
refunding such payments and dealing with complaints and queries relating to such payments and
refunds. You can find information about the payment services providers' privacy policies and
practices at [https://stripe.com/privacy](https://stripe.com/privacy) (Stripe).

3.3 We may disclose your inquiry data to one or more of those selected third party suppliers of
goods and services identified on our website for the purpose of market research so that they can
offer, market and sell to you relevant goods and/or services. Each such third party will act as a
data controller in relation to the inquiry data that we supply to it; and upon contacting you, each
such third party will supply to you a copy of its own privacy policy, which will govern that third
party's use of your personal data.

3.4 In addition to the specific disclosures of personal data set out in this Section 3, we may
disclose your personal data where such disclosure is necessary for compliance with a legal
obligation to which we are subject, or in order to protect your vital interests or the vital
interests of another natural person.

## 5. Retaining and deleting personal data

5.1 This Section 5 sets out our data retention policies and procedure, which are designed to help
ensure that we comply with our legal obligations in relation to the retention and deletion of
personal data.

5.2 Personal data that we process for any purpose or purposes shall not be kept for longer than is
necessary for that purpose or those purposes.

5.3 We will retain your personal data as follows:

1. Account data, profile data, and notification data will be retained for a minimum period of one
   month following the date of registration and will continue to be maintained until the account is
   terminated by the user. If the user does not finish registration by purchasing a badge within the
   one month period, the account will automatically be deleted.

5.4 In some cases it is not possible for us to specify in advance the periods for which your
personal data will be retained. In such cases, we will determine the period of retention based on
the following criteria:

5.4.1 the period of retention of service data will be determined based on our legitimate interests
in maintaining historical references and statistical information regarding usage of our services.
Data may become aggregated where no personally identifiable information can be extracted from
statistical information.

5.4.2 the period of retention of publication data will be determined based on the original creator’s
actions. By default, publication of posts and/or comments will not expire unless the original
creator removes them, an administrative member receives a request to remove them by the original
creator, or an administrative member removes them as an act of moderation.

5.4.3 the period of retention of inquiry data will be determined based on the requirements of the
request, survey, or otherwise by which inquiry data is collected. Data may be immediately aggregated
or become aggregated at a later time where no personally identifiable information can be extracted
from results.

5.5 Notwithstanding the other provisions of this Section 6, we may retain your personal data where
such retention is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation to which we are subject, or in
order to protect your vital interests or the vital interests of another natural person.
