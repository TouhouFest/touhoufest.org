## Volunteering

TouhouFest is built on volunteer participation, so we encourage every attendee to get involved. This
page will tell you what you need to do if you’d like to join the TouhouFest team. The info here is
applicable to new volunteers, or returning volunteers.

## Perks

Volunteers are required to purchase admission to the event, but are eligible for various rewards,
including food, and badge refunds. The more hours you put in, the more rewards you get! Some hours
are weighted more than others (such as high skill jobs, or late night shifts), so you can choose a
schedule that works best for you. The exact perks are still being deliberated so please wait warmly!

## How to Volunteer

### Step 1: Begin the Registration Process

We’ll need to begin collecting your information, so go to the
[pre-registration page](https://registration.touhoufest.org/preregistration) where you can begin
entering your basic details.

<img src="{{ <img_url path="img/volunteering/step1.webp" /> }}" alt="Pre-registration form
screenshot" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

### Step 2: Check the “Want to Volunteer?” Checkbox

<img src="{{ <img_url path="img/volunteering/step2.webp" /> }}" alt="Want to Volunteer checkbox
screenshot" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

The checkbox appears about half-way down the page. Once you check this, additional options will
become available.

### Step 3: Select the areas you want to help

<img src="{{ <img_url path="img/volunteering/step3.webp" /> }}" alt="Volunteer department selection
screenshot" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

TouhouFest has several departments working together in tight coordination to keep every fest running
smoothly. Check off whichever department interests you most! We recommend choosing no more than
three different departments, since learning new departments can be a challenging process. Selections
don’t guarantee that you will be placed in these departments; department heads will review requests
and make assignments accordingly.

If you’re not sure what goes on in each department, hover your mouse over (or tap on mobile) the
circled-i next to each for a short description.

### Step 4: Complete and pay for your registration

<img src="{{ <img_url path="img/volunteering/step4.webp" /> }}" alt="Registration payment
screenshot" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

If you work enough hours at-fest, we’ll refund your registration as soon as possible following the
event!

### Step 5: You’re all confirmed!

That’s all you need to do to register yourself as a volunteer! From here, the departments you’re
assigned to may have their own individual procedures, as some of them choose to optionally send a
“welcome to our department” e-mail to their volunteers. Either way, as the event approaches (usually
2-3 months beforehand), our Staff Resources department will send you a Volunteer Checklist where you
can confirm your attendance, specify food preferences, and most importantly, sign up for shifts in
your department.

If you have any questions about the above, feel free to contact our Staff Resources Department at
[hr@touhoufest.org](mailto:hr@touhoufest.org).
